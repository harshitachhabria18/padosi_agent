Want to guarantee you stay at the very top of the search results? Our Smart Rank Algorithm constantly calculates a dynamic score for your profile. Follow these rules to maximize your points and secure the #1 spot!

1. The "Fast Responder" Bonus (Up to 50 Pts)

Customers prefer active agents. We heavily reward agents who log in to their dashboard frequently!

  • Logged in within the last 3 days: +50 Points 🚀
  • Logged in within the last 2 weeks: +25 Points
  • Inactive for over 30 days: 0 Points 📉 (Your rank will drop severely)
2. Profile Completion Bonus (Up to 30 Pts)

You gain an automatic +5 Points for every single one of the following you fill out in your settings:

Profile Photo Office Address Experience Years WhatsApp Number License Number Languages
3. Customer Review Score (Up to 10 Pts)

If you maintain an Average Rating of 4.5 Stars or Higher from verified customers, you receive an instantaneous algorithmic boost over peers who have poor ratings.

4. Performance Base Score (Up to 60 Pts)

Automated ranking points are naturally distributed based on your career volume:

  • Experience Years: Up to 20 Points
  • Total Client Base: Up to 20 Points
  • Claims Processed: Up to 20 Points
5. Official Badges (Up to 15 Pts)

Earning official platform Badges immediately signals authority to the algorithm and explicitly adds an instant flat +15 Points to your total search visibility score.

6. The Perfect Match Multiplier (Up to 30 Pts)

When a user specifically searches for an Insurance Type, agents who explicitly list that exact segment in their profile receive an immense +30 Point Multiplier, ensuring they climb directly to the top specifically for that customer!
